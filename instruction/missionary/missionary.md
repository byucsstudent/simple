# Church ministry

**Discipline:** Eternal Progression
**Problem type:** Preaching the gospel of Jesus Christ
**Difficulty:** 3 / 5
**Modes:** Practice
**Instability:** Off

## Learning Outcomes

If you're preparing a ministry plan or sermon series, you could state the outcomes like this:

1. People understand and respond to the gospel of Jesus Christ.
2. New believers begin a lifelong journey of discipleship.
3.Christians grow in faith, obedience, and Christlike character.
4.The church becomes more effective in evangelism and disciple-making.
5. Believers demonstrate God's love through service, compassion, and holy living.
6. God is glorified as lives, families, and communities are transformed by the power of the gospel.
