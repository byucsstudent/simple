# Woodworker

**Discipline:** Skilled trade
**Job title:** Custom Cabinet Maker
**Difficulty:** 1 / 5
**Modes:** Practice

## Job Description

Join our team of dedicated woodworkers in a fast-paced custom cabinetry shop. As an entry-level Custom Cabinet Maker, you will work alongside seasoned craftsmen to produce high-quality, bespoke cabinetry and architectural millwork for residential and commercial projects. This role is designed for individuals eager to build a career in the skilled trades, offering hands-on experience in every stage of the fabrication process from raw lumber to finished installation-ready products.

## Responsibilities

*   Assist senior cabinet makers with the assembly of cabinet boxes, face frames, and drawer units using various joinery techniques.
*   Perform meticulous sanding and surface preparation of wood components to ensure a high-quality finish.
*   Operate basic shop machinery, such as table saws, miter saws, and drill presses, under direct supervision and in strict accordance with safety protocols.
*   Install functional hardware, including hinges, drawer slides, and decorative pulls, ensuring precise alignment and smooth operation.
*   Maintain a clean and organized workspace by managing sawdust collection, disposing of scrap material, and organizing inventory.
*   Assist with the loading, unloading, and safe transport of finished cabinetry and materials to job sites or delivery vehicles.

## Requirements

*   High school diploma or equivalent; completion of a vocational woodworking program or basic shop class is preferred.
*   Demonstrated ability to read a tape measure accurately to 1/16th of an inch and perform basic shop math calculations.
*   Strong mechanical aptitude and a basic level of comfort working with hand tools and power tools.
*   Physical stamina to stand for eight or more hours per day and the ability to lift materials weighing up to 50 pounds.
*   A strong work ethic, punctuality, and a coachable attitude with a genuine desire to master the trade of custom cabinetry.
*   Excellent attention to detail and the ability to follow complex verbal and written instructions in a loud shop environment.

## Learning Outcomes

- Represent yourself professionally
- Demonstrate ability to be responsible
- Demonstrate reliability
- Collaborative
- Curious and creative
