# Energy Solutions

**Discipline:** Electrical Engineering
**Job title:** Energy storage solutions engineer
**Difficulty:** 3 / 5
**Modes:** Practice

## Job Description

As a key member of our Power Systems Engineering team, the Energy Storage Solutions Engineer will lead the technical development and integration of utility-scale Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS). This role involves guiding projects from initial feasibility and conceptual design through to commissioning and grid interconnection. You will work at the forefront of the energy transition, collaborating with cross-functional teams to deliver reliable, high-performance storage assets that support grid stability and renewable energy integration.

#### Responsibilities

* Lead the electrical design and engineering of utility-scale BESS projects, including the development of single-line diagrams (SLDs), equipment layouts, and detailed BOS (Balance of System) specifications.
* Conduct comprehensive power system studies, including load flow, short circuit, coordination, and arc flash analysis using industry-standard software such as ETAP or SKM.
* Develop and optimize control strategies and Energy Management System (EMS) architectures to ensure seamless integration with existing grid infrastructure and microgrid controllers.
* Manage technical relationships with battery OEMs, inverter manufacturers, and EPC partners to ensure equipment selection aligns with project performance guarantees and safety standards.
* Provide senior-level technical oversight during the construction and commissioning phases, troubleshooting complex field issues and validating system performance against design benchmarks.
* Ensure all designs and installations comply with relevant industry codes and standards, including NFPA 855, UL 9540, IEEE 1547, and local interconnection requirements.
* Mentor junior engineers and contribute to the development of internal standardized engineering processes and technical best practices.

#### Requirements

* Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering with a concentration in Power Systems or Renewable Energy.
* 6–10+ years of experience in electrical engineering, with at least 3 years of direct experience in the design and implementation of large-scale energy storage or solar-plus-storage projects.
* Advanced proficiency in power system modeling and simulation tools (e.g., PSS/E, PSCAD, DigSILENT, or ETAP) and CAD software.
* Deep technical knowledge of power conversion systems (PCS), battery chemistry characteristics, and high-voltage substation interface requirements.
* Demonstrated experience navigating the utility interconnection process and a firm grasp of regional ISO/RTO market rules and technical requirements.
* Professional Engineer (PE) license is strongly preferred, or the ability to obtain licensure within six months of hire.

## Learning Outcomes

- Demonstrates capacity
- Demonstrates curiosity
- Demonstrates collaboration
