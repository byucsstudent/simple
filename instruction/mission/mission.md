# Mission

**Discipline:** Eternal Progression
**Problem type:** Serving a mission for the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints
**Difficulty:** 3 / 5
**Modes:** Practice
**Instability:** Off

## Learning Outcomes

Serving a mission for **The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints** is a sacred opportunity for spiritual growth and discipleship. As you strive to serve the Lord, periods of significant growth are often accompanied by mental, emotional, physical, and spiritual challenges. These experiences can strengthen your faith, deepen your reliance on Jesus Christ, and refine your character. As you serve a mission, you seek the following outcomes:

1. Develop a deeper and more personal relationship with Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.
1. Strengthen your testimony of the restored gospel through study, prayer, and faithful service.
1. Become a more devoted disciple of Jesus Christ by living His teachings every day.
1. Invite others to come unto Christ by helping them exercise faith, repent, make and keep sacred covenants, and receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.
1. Develop Christlike attributes such as charity, humility, patience, diligence, obedience, and courage.
1. Learn to recognize and follow the promptings of the Holy Ghost.
1. Increase your capacity to love, serve, and minister to individuals and families.
1. Build habits of daily scripture study, sincere prayer, and personal revelation that will bless you throughout your life.
1. Develop resilience by facing challenges with faith, hope, and trust in the Lord.
1. Learn to work effectively with companions, members, leaders, and those you teach.
1. Improve your communication, teaching, leadership, and problem-solving abilities.
1. Honor your covenants and prepare for a lifetime of faithful service in your family, the Church, and your community.
1. Help establish and strengthen the Lord's Church by finding, teaching, baptizing, and fellowshipping those who choose to follow Jesus Christ.
1. Return home with a stronger testimony, greater spiritual maturity, and a lifelong commitment to follow the Savior.

These outcomes emphasize that missionary service is not only about helping others come unto Christ, but also about becoming more like Him through faithful service and reliance on His grace.
