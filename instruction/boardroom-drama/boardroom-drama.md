# Boardroom Drama

**Discipline:** Business Management
**Problem type:** A board meeting to discuss a startup's future
**Difficulty:** 1 / 5
**Modes:** Practice
**Instability:** Off

## Learning Outcomes

The board for a startup is meeting to discuss the future of the company and must decide to 1) continue operations, 2) seek for a buyer, or 3) close down operations.

Learning outcomes include:
- The ability to decided the financial stability of the company
- The ability to access the ability of the management team to be successful
- The ability to access the current product value and market opportunities
